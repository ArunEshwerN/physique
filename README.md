# Physique

A twelve-month home training and nutrition program for a lean, athletic build — wide
shoulders, a full upper chest, lat width, visible abs. Built around one adjustable bench,
two dumbbells, a pull-up bar and 64 kg of cast iron. Four sessions a week, none longer than
an hour.

## The short version

- **Phase 0, weeks 1–4** — full body 3×/week, deliberately easy, so week one doesn't end the
  whole project.
- **Phase 1, weeks 5–16** — Upper A / Lower A / Upper B / Lower B, Monday through Friday.
- **Progression** — double progression: climb the reps to the top of the range on every set,
  then add 2 kg per dumbbell and start again.
- **Deload** every eight weeks. Swap to Block 2 exercises at week 18. Repeat indefinitely.
- **Nutrition** — four palm-sized protein servings a day, +250 kcal, gain 0.5–0.75 kg/month,
  stop at 70 kg.
- **The log is the program.** Double progression cannot be run from memory.

## Equipment

Everything is Decathlon, 28 mm standard. Two 45 cm **threaded** bars (ID 8766096) — the
locknuts thread onto the bar itself, so there's no 15 kg vertical limit to worry about when
pressing overhead. Cast iron plates only: 8 × 1 kg, 8 × 2 kg, 8 × 5 kg. That's 34.3 kg per
dumbbell at full load, which is a three-to-five-year ceiling and more than this goal will
ever ask for.
